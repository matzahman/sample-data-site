How this works

  1. Family fills out the form

    The existing Jotform intake stays as it is. Families choose whether their submission is counted on this site.

  2. Identifying details are stripped

    Names, dates of birth, case numbers, addresses, phone numbers and emails are removed automatically. County is kept; exact dates become a month.

  3. Sorted into categories

    Each account is tagged with case type, the issues it describes, the roles involved and the outcome. A volunteer checks every tag.

  4. Counted on the dashboard

    Only the tags and counts appear here, with small groups hidden. The original forms never leave the private system.

A family can ask to be removed at any time; their record disappears from every count at the next update.
